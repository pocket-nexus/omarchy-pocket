Smoothness

The iPhone Duo refreshes at up to 120 Hz on a chip that scores 4,722. On the RG DS Plus, Omarchy Pocket moves windows at 60 fps on a chip that scores 176, with 557 MB of its 953 MB free while Tailscale and mihomo run. It replaces the desktop graphics stack with one PocketJS process. Both are smooth. One had less to work with.

What $1,999 buys instead

21 RG DS Plus units: 84 cores, 21 GB of memory, 42 screens, 7.2 kg. Their Geekbench multi-core scores add up to 10,395, which is not how Geekbench works, and is still less than one iPhone Duo.

Recommendation

Best computer

iPhone Duo. It is faster, lighter and thinner, lasts longer and has a camera and a modem. It should: it costs 21× as much.

Best value for opening YouTube

RG DS Plus. It also opens X and GitHub, and joins your tailnet. It has a D-pad.
