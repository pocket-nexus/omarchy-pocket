The real shell, in your browser.

This is the same bundle the RG DS Plus runs, on PocketJS's core compiled to WebAssembly. The top screen is the monitor, the bottom screen is the keyboard, the gamepad is SUPER.

The daemon under it is a stand-in: the terminal is a small script, the panels show sample data, and applets and voice input run on the device.
